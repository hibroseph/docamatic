Sourdough basics

A beginner's sourdough, step by step

By Jo Baker · 12 min read

Good sourdough asks for patience more than skill. Feed your starter the night before, and use it when it has doubled and smells pleasantly sour.

1. Mix and rest

Combine 500 g flour with 350 g water and let it rest for an hour. This rest, the autolyse, makes the dough easier to shape later.

2. Stretch and fold

Every 30 minutes for two hours, lift one side of the dough and fold it over. Four sets are usually enough.

Then let it rise until it has grown by about half. In a warm kitchen this takes four hours; in a cool one, closer to eight.
