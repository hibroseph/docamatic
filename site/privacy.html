Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Docamatic is a browser extension that lets you add sticky notes to webpages. This policy explains what the extension does with your information. In short: your notes stay on your computer. We collect usage statistics and error reports to understand how the extension is used and keep it working, and you can turn the usage statistics off.

Your notes

Everything you write in Docamatic (note text, titles, tags, colors and positions) and the address of the page each note belongs to (including the full address it was created on, with any passwords, tokens or other credentials removed) is stored only in your browser, using the browser's extension storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

To show your notes, the extension checks the address of the page you are on and compares it with the pages you have notes on. This happens entirely inside your browser.

Uninstalling the extension deletes your notes from your browser.

Usage statistics

To understand how many people use Docamatic and which features are used, the extension sends usage events to PostHog, an analytics service. These events include:

  • When the extension is installed or updated, and the previous version
  • When the popup or side panel is opened and which section of it is used (for example search or settings)
  • When a note is created, edited, deleted or tagged (the fact that it happened, not the content)
  • Once a day, counts of how many notes, pages, tags and favorites you have
  • The extension version
  • When Docamatic is installed: whether this website was open in another tab, and the campaign labels (such as utm_source) on the Chrome Web Store page you installed from, if it had any. Only those yes/no answers and labels are sent, never the addresses of your tabs.

Each event is tied to a random ID created when the extension is installed. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account. Usage events never include your note text, the addresses or names of websites you visit, or your browsing history.

Turning it off: open the Docamatic popup, go to Settings and uncheck "Share usage statistics". No usage events are sent after that.

Error reports

When something goes wrong in the extension, an error report is sent to Sentry, an error monitoring service, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report includes the error message and where in the extension's code it happened, the extension version, your browser and operating system version, and the names of recent actions inside the extension (such as "note added"). If the error happened on a webpage, the report includes that website's domain name (for example example.com), but never the full page address.

Error reports never include your note text. They are sent even when usage statistics are turned off, because they are needed to keep the extension working.

IP addresses

Like any request over the internet, sending usage events and error reports reveals your IP address to PostHog and Sentry, and both services store it with the event or report. It is used to estimate your approximate location (such as country and city) and to tell apart problems affecting different users. We don't use it to identify you or combine it with any other information about you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent any data.
  • We don't use data for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone other than the services named above, which process it on our behalf.
  • We don't use data to determine creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to running and improving Docamatic.

When you uninstall

After you uninstall Docamatic, Chrome opens a short, optional feedback form hosted on Google Forms. Filling it in is entirely up to you; anything you submit is covered by Google's privacy policy.

This website

This website counts visits with PostHog: which page was opened, the page you came from, your browser and screen size, and clicks on the "Add to Chrome" buttons and the demo. Nothing is stored on your device (no cookies), each visit gets a new random id, and nothing you type into the demo notes is sent. Like any website you visit, PostHog receives your IP address, which it uses to estimate your country and city.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Questions or requests? Email sticknoteapp@gmail.com.
