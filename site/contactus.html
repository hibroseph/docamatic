If something isn't working

These help a lot:

  • What you did, and what happened instead
  • The website it happened on, if you're comfortable sharing it
  • Your browser and its version (in Chrome: menu, Help, About Google Chrome)
  • The Docamatic version, at the bottom of Settings in the Docamatic popup

If your notes look wrong after an update, don't worry: Docamatic keeps automatic backups. Open Settings, then Backup & restore, before changing anything.
