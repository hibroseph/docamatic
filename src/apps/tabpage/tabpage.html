Sticky notes for the web.

  1. 1

    Keep it handy

    Click the puzzle piece at the right end of the toolbar, then the pin next to Docamatic. Now is always one click away.

  2. 2

    Add a note on any website

    Go to the page, click in the toolbar, then the note button at the top of the popup (or Create New Note). Your note appears on the page.

  3. 3

    Find it again

    Go back to the page and your note is waiting in the same spot. Click any time to search all your notes, see your favorites, or sort them by date or website.
