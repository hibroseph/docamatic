Add to Chrome, free

These are real notes: drag one by its colored top bar, click in it to write, and use ⋮ to change its color.

No account. Your notes stay on your computer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open any page

    A recipe, a paper, a pull request, a video. If you can read it in Chrome, you can put a note on it.

  2. 2

    Add a note

    Click Docamatic in your toolbar, then the note button at the top. Drag the note beside the part it's about.

  3. 3

    Come back to it

    Next time you open the page, your note is waiting in the same spot. On YouTube, it's on that one video.

Find it again

Click Docamatic to see the notes for the page you're on. The rest are close by.

  • Search everything you've written, on every site.
  • Tag notes to group them across sites: “recipes”, “to read”, “thesis”.
  • Favorites for the few you keep coming back to.
  • Sort by date or by website.

The small things
