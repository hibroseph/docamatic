Sticky notes for the web.

Put a note on any page, right next to what you're reading. It's still there when you come back, and everything you've written is one search away.

Add to Chrome, free

No account. Your notes stay on your computer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open any page

    A recipe, a paper, a pull request, a video. If you can read it in Chrome, you can mark it up.

  2. 2

    Add a note

    Click Docamatic and New note, or press Alt+Shift+N. Drag it beside the part it's about.

  3. 3

    Come back to it

    Next time you open the page, your note is waiting in the same spot. On YouTube, it's on that one video.

Stays with what it's about

Web apps often scroll one part of the screen, not the whole page: your inbox, a chat, a document's sidebar. A note you put there scrolls with it, and tucks out of sight with the content it's next to. Put one on a part that never moves, and it stays put.

Scroll the message list to try it. In Docamatic it's a setting: Move notes with scrolling panels.

Find it again

Docamatic shows the notes for the page you're on, and keeps the rest close by.

  • Search everything. Titles, text, tags and page names, even with a typo.
  • Tag notes to group them across sites: “recipes”, “to read”, “thesis”. Rename or recolor a tag once and it changes everywhere.
  • Favorites for the few you keep coming back to.
  • Sort by newest, recently edited, or website.

The small things

Undo
Deleted a note by accident? Undo is right there.
Shortcut
Alt+Shift+N adds a note without opening anything.
Light or dark
Follows your computer, or pick one in Settings.
Your colors
Six note colors. Notes from older versions keep theirs.
Right-click
“Add a note here” puts it right where you clicked, with any text you selected.
Out of the way
Collapse a note to its title, set its size, or keep it on screen while you scroll.

Yours, and only yours

Your notes are saved in your browser, on your computer. They aren't uploaded anywhere, and there's no account to make. Docamatic keeps automatic backups, and you can download one whenever you like from Settings.
