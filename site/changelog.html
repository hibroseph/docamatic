What's new

Every update to Docamatic, newest first.

5.1.0

Coming soon
  • A whole new look: calmer notes, a cleaner popup, and this website
  • Search all your notes from the popup, even with a typo, and filter by tag
  • Tabs in the popup for this page, all notes (sort by newest, oldest, recently edited or website), favorites and tags
  • Manage your tags in one place: see how many notes have each, rename or recolor a tag everywhere, or delete it
  • Undo after deleting a note
  • "Find on page" in the popup takes you straight to a note and points it out
  • On pages that load more as you scroll, the popup tells you when a note is further down, and "Bring here" moves it to where you are
  • Right-click a page and choose "Add a note here", with the text you selected already in it
  • Collapse a note to its title (double-click its handle), drag its corner to set its height, or keep it on screen while you scroll
  • Choose the text size of notes in Settings
  • Notes on Amazon products stay with the product, however you got to its page
  • Spell check underlines only while you're typing in a note
  • New notes appear beside what you're reading, with the cursor ready to type
  • Keyboard shortcut for a new note: Alt+Shift+N
  • Choose a light or dark theme, and the color for new notes, in Settings
  • Lists show page titles instead of web addresses
  • Preview: notes in a side panel next to the page you're reading
  • Preview: a setting to move notes with scrolling parts of web apps, like an inbox
  • All your notes are still where you left them, in the colors you gave them

5.0.0

October 19, 2026
  • Docamatic is back! Rebuilt for Chrome's current extension platform (Manifest V3)
  • Your notes from the previous version are carried over automatically
  • Much lighter on browsing: the notes display only loads on pages that have notes
  • Notes look the same on every site, without the site's styles affecting them (or theirs being affected)
  • Notes keep saving reliably when Chrome puts the extension to sleep in the background
  • Fixed notes not showing on some sites, including after navigating within sites like X and YouTube
  • Fixed the "sort by date" view and many smaller bugs
  • Security improvements, including protecting your notes from the websites they are on
  • Added a tutorial page on first install
  • Added usage statistics to see how Docamatic is used (turn off anytime in Settings) and a privacy policy

Known issues in the 2020 version

  • Notes cannot be added to all webpages. We are slowly expanding our ability to add to all webpages
  • The style changes on certain webpages
  • If you change the size of the note on the left or the top, it will result in the note shifting the next time you view it.
  • Weird behavior can be had on pages that are SPA's
